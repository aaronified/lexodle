# How to play

Guess the hidden word. After each guess every letter is marked:

- **green**: right letter, right place
- **yellow**: in the word, wrong place
- **grey**: not in the word

## Game modes and settings

The home screen has the language, the daily streak, **achievements** and **how to play**, and the
last row: your **playstyle** (a box with its icon; it opens the history), **play**, **daily** and
**game modes** (the grid). On a phone the last row sticks to the bottom of the screen; on a desktop it
is the last row of the page. The gear at the top right is **settings**. Game modes holds the mode, the
letters, the difficulty, shared letters and the helper, with play, the solver and learn. Settings holds
the theme, backup, updates (Android), support and about.

| Setting    | Options |
| ---------- | ------- |
| Language   | English (US and UK spelling), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Turkish, Romanian, Czech, Indonesian, Hungarian, Catalan; each with its flag |
| Letters    | 4 to 8 |
| Difficulty | a grid: tries (easy 8, normal 6, hard 4, brutal 3) by word pool: answers drawn from the 500, 1,500, 3,000 or 6,000 most common words of that length. A pool level is offered only if the language has at least 1,000 more words of that length than the level below it, and the last level offered takes every word left, so none is wasted: English at five letters has 4,994 words, so its "obscure" row is all 4,994; Danish and Indonesian never get "obscure" |

The letters and the helper are sliders: use the arrows, swipe or drag (the value slides and fades
with your finger), scroll sideways or hold shift and scroll the wheel, or click one and use the
arrow keys. Type on a keyboard or use the on-screen one, which adds each language's accented
letters. The up and down arrow keys scroll whatever the mouse is over, as the wheel does.

## Blanks

Not sure of a letter? Type a blank with the `_` key (or the space bar) to pencil in a pattern
like `a_ode`. Tap a tile, or use the left and right arrow keys, to move the cursor; typing fills
the selected tile and moves on to the next one that is not a letter. A blank can be typed but not
submitted, so it never reaches the scoring.

## Modes

**Classic** is one word. In a **quartet** four words are hidden and every guess is played on all
four boards at once (the keyboard splits each key in four, one part per board); a **relay race**
plays the four boards one after the other, each starting with every earlier guess already
applied. Quartet and relay race get extra tries (3 and 4), and the *shared letters* setting says
how often their words may share a letter. The helper shows in classic only, so it is greyed out
for the others, and shared letters is greyed out in classic.

## The helper and the review

The **helper** setting chooses what the game tells you while you play: *off*, *skill only*,
*skill + luck*, or *full help* (which adds how many possible words remain). When a game ends, the
**review** steps through your guesses one at a time (buttons, the progress bar, or the arrow
keys). Each step shows the board with a table of skill, luck, words left and info gained; your
guess beside the best picks at that point; how the guess divided the pool of possible words,
against the pool you landed in; and a short reading of the step. Your guess can be a valid word
that the game never uses as an answer; the review says so, and nothing more.

When a game ends, the answer's pronunciation (IPA and audio), meaning, synonyms and antonyms are
fetched from [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org), with a link to the full entry. If
Wiktionary has no entry or is unreachable, the game carries on without it.

See [Skill, luck and daring](Skill-luck-and-daring) for what the numbers mean.
