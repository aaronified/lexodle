# Lexodle wiki

Lexodle is a minimal word game in 17 Latin-script languages. Try the browser demo at
<https://aaronified.github.io/lexodle/> or install the Android APK from the
[releases](https://github.com/aaronified/lexodle/releases/latest). The [README](https://github.com/aaronified/lexodle#readme)
has the highlights; these pages have the detail.

**Playing**
- [How to play](How-to-play): the rules, the settings, blanks, the three modes
- [Daily word](Daily-word): one word a day, the streak, the trusted clock
- [Solver and learn mode](Solver-and-learn-mode): help for a game played elsewhere, and practising the best guess

**Your play**
- [Skill, luck and daring](Skill-luck-and-daring): how a guess is scored, and the review
- [History and playstyles](History-and-playstyles): the history, the charts, the six playstyles
- [Languages](Languages): everything above is kept per language, with its flag
- [Achievements](Achievements)

**Behind it**
- [Word lists](Word-lists): where the words come from and how they are filtered
- [Offline and privacy](Offline-and-privacy): nothing is fetched to draw
- [Updates and support](Updates-and-support): updating the Android app, and chipping in
- [Developing](Developing): build, test, screenshots, deploy, file map
- [The maths](https://github.com/aaronified/lexodle/tree/main/docs/paper): the paper, with proofs and experiments
