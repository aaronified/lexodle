# Languages

There are 17 languages: English (`en`, US and UK), Español (`es`), Français (`fr`), Deutsch (`de`),
Italiano (`it`), Português (`pt`, Brazil and Portugal), Nederlands (`nl`), Polski (`pl`), Svenska
(`sv`), Dansk (`da`), Norsk bokmål (`nb`), Türkçe (`tr`), Română (`ro`), Čeština (`cs`), Indonesia
(`id`), Magyar (`hu`) and Català (`ca`). Each has its flag; English and Portuguese show two. A word
is looked up in the Wiktionary of its own language (Norsk on no.wiktionary.org, English on
en.wiktionary.org).

Each language has its own everything. For the language being played (and the one a page is
showing, picked with the row of flags):

- the **history**, its overall analysis and its charts;
- the **playstyle**, read from that language's games, and the styles held there;
- the **daily streak**, its best, and its calendar;
- the **achievements**, with their progress.

Only a few things span languages: the achievements that count languages (bilingual, polyglot, six
tongues, nine tongues), the language-mastery and completionist badges (the top level of
completionist is the tower of babel) and every learn-mode achievement, which is kept once for you. Every achievement wears the flag of its language; the ones
that count languages wear the flags of the languages they count.

**Older data.** What was kept before each language had its own (one playstyle, one streak, one set
of achievements) was moved to the language that was being played when the app first opened with
this version. A backup from before is read the same way, as the language it was made in. Backups
are version 2, with a section per language.
