Flags

The flags of 18 countries (us, gb, es, fr, de, it, br, pt, nl, pl, se, dk, no, tr,
ro, cz, id, hu) are the Twemoji graphics, from https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji
(assets/svg), copyright Twitter, Inc and other contributors, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), the text of which follows.
They are unchanged apart from the viewBox, which is cropped to the flag itself.

The Catalan flag (ca.svg) is drawn for this project.

They are kept in the app, not fetched: the flag of a language used to be an emoji,
which needs an emoji font the device may not have (Windows draws none at all) and
which a web page fetches from a font server.
