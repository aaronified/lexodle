# Wiktionary entries

The meanings, examples, pronunciation, origins and synonyms shown for a word are taken from the
Wiktionary edition of the word's language (English, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Catalan,
Romanian, German, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Turkish and
Indonesian), at wiktionary.org. Wiktionary text is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The entries are fetched when needed, cut to the parts the app shows and laid out in its own way. A
word you save is kept as a copy of its entry with your own notes: the copy stays on your phone (or in
your browser) and in the backup files you make, so it opens without a connection. Every entry links
to its Wiktionary page, where its authors and history are listed.

Pronunciation recordings are played from Wikimedia Commons under the licence each recording states
on its Wiktionary page; they are not stored in the app.
